Admissions Information

Application Process

1. Visit the school or call for enquiry
2. Collect and complete the application form
3. Submit required documents
4. Assessment / Interview (for some levels)
5. Admission confirmation & fee payment

Required Documents

• Birth Certificate
• Previous School Report (if applicable)
• Passport-sized Photos (4)
• Immunization Records
• Parent / Guardian ID

School Fees

We offer competitive and affordable fees with flexible payment plans. Contact our admissions office for the detailed fee structure and any available scholarships or discounts.
