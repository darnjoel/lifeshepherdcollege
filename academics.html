Academic Programs

Comprehensive education from Crèche to Primary 6, building strong foundations for lifelong success

Home / Academics

Crèche & Nursery

Ages 1 - 4

Our crèche and nursery program provides a warm, safe, and stimulating environment for our youngest learners. Through structured play, sensory exploration, and nurturing care, we support early cognitive, emotional, and social development.

Focus Areas: Sensory Play, Motor Skills Development, Basic Language & Communication, Social Interaction, Creative Expression, Music & Movement

Kindergarten (KG1 & KG2)

Ages 4 - 6

Our kindergarten program bridges early childhood to formal education. Using play-based and activity-centred learning, we build foundational literacy, numeracy, and social skills that prepare children for primary school success.

Subjects: Phonics, Basic Mathematics, Environmental Studies, Creative Arts, Physical Education, Religious & Moral Education

Lower Primary (P1 - P3)

Ages 6 - 9

The lower primary program strengthens foundational knowledge with structured instruction in core subjects. Students develop reading fluency, mathematical reasoning, and scientific curiosity in an engaging learning environment.

Subjects: English Language, Mathematics, Natural Science, Our World Our People, French Language, Creative Arts, Physical Education, Religious & Moral Education, ICT

Upper Primary (P4 - P6)

Ages 9 - 12

Our upper primary program prepares students for the transition to Junior High School. With deepened subject coverage and critical thinking emphasis, students are equipped for academic advancement and lifelong learning.

Subjects: English Language, Mathematics, Integrated Science, Our World Our People, French Language, ICT, French, Creative Arts, Physical Education, Religious & Moral Education

Beyond the Classroom

Special Programs & Activities

ICT Education

Computer literacy and basic digital skills training to prepare pupils for the modern digital world.

French Language

Introduction to French language to broaden cultural horizons and communication skills.

Music & Cultural Arts

Music, traditional dance, drama, and visual arts programs to nurture creativity and cultural appreciation.

Football, athletics, and various indoor and outdoor games that promote physical fitness and teamwork.

Annual speech, prize-giving, and graduation ceremonies to celebrate our pupils' academic distinction and character development.
